What students do

  1. Sign in with first and last name, your last name, and period. Remind them to use the same name every time, or their scores split into two rows.
  2. Pick a domain, read its Key Ideas, and answer all of its questions. Domains have 9 to 18 questions, 3 per standard element.
  3. Submit. They see which questions they got right or wrong and a status for each skill, but never the correct answers.
  4. Retake if you allow it. Their newest attempt replaces the older one in the Gradebook, and every attempt stays in Responses.

Students need scratch paper for Punnett squares (Domain 2) and the cladogram sketch (Domain 3).

Reading the spreadsheet

GradebookSee one row per student with a 0-3 score on each element (SB1a to SB6e) and a percent for each domain. Turn on the filter to show only your class.
Needs HelpBuild reteach groups. Every student below 3/3 on a skill, with the questions they missed. Filter by Teacher and Period, then sort by Element.
Element SummarySpot whole-class reteach needs. A skill under 40% Secure is a reteach for everyone, not a small group.
ResponsesSee the raw record of every submission. Leave this tab alone; the other three rebuild from it.

If something goes wrong

  • A student's scores are missing. Check Responses for a spelling of their name or a different period. Their results page says "Not sent yet" if they lost internet; it resends on its own once they reconnect with the guide open.
  • A student shows up twice. They typed their name two ways. Fix the name in Responses, then use Biology Guide > Rebuild dashboards in the Sheet's menu.
  • A student switched computers. Answers save in the browser, so they start fresh on a new device. Scores already submitted are safe in the Sheet.
